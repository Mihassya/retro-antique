# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS (Vite). Deploy: Vercel, public GitHub repo (portfolio).

## Users

Primary: buyers and gift-givers in Ust-Kamenogorsk looking for an object for their home or a present
that nobody else will give. They browse on a phone, often before a visit, and need to see that the
shop has interesting things and how to get there. Secondary: collectors (coins, porcelain) and people
who want to sell an old item (buy-back is a real but secondary scenario).

## Product Purpose

Website for «Ретро», an antique shop at ul. Kazakhstan 77, Ust-Kamenogorsk, open since 2006.
The site makes people want to come in, shows what kind of things are on the shelves and their prices,
and makes contact (WhatsApp, visit) effortless. There is no online checkout.

## Positioning

One room, one antiquarian (Alexander), twenty years at the same address. Things are bought and sold
by a person who knows each object's story, not a marketplace listing.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No payment online; reserve or ask via WhatsApp, buy in the shop.
- Hours 10:00–18:00. Payment in shop: cash, card transfer, QR.
- Buy-back: selective, item is evaluated in the shop.
- Built as a gift by the owner's son; the owner's materials (photos, phone, texts) can be used.
- Portfolio piece: craft and motion quality matter.

## Brand Commitments

Name «Ретро» is fixed. No existing logo or visual identity; logo is to be designed.

## Evidence on Hand

- Price list from 2GIS (13 real items with prices in ₸): Soviet porcelain figurines (Polonne),
  bronze «Добрый кот» 115 000 ₸, cast iron, Old China (teapot «Стебли бамбука», Amitabha), busts.
- 2GIS rating 4.7 from 44 ratings; real reviews (e.g. a visitor since 2006).
- No product photography yet; owner can supply. Until then, open-collection images, labelled.

## Product Principles

1. Objects first: every screen shows a real thing, not decoration.
2. A person behind the counter: the voice is Alexander's, warm and knowledgeable.
3. Getting there is part of the product: address, hours and WhatsApp are never more than a tap away.
4. Honest prices where they exist.
